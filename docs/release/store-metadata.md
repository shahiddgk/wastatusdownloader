- **Full Description (max 4,000 chars):**
```text
Keeva is a clean, private, and modern media utility designed to give you effortless control over ephemeral status photos and videos.

Built with a privacy-first mindset, Keeva works entirely on your device without user accounts, cloud uploads, advertisements, or background tracking.

KEY FEATURES:

• Instant Moments Radar: Effortlessly browse active status media from your selected storage folder with intelligent chronological sorting and freshness badges.
• Crystal-Clear Media Viewer: High-fidelity image zoom and seamless full-screen video playback with hardware-accelerated controls.
• Kept Vault: Save your favorite memories permanently into your local vault so you never lose them when statuses expire.
• Zero Ads, Zero Bloat: Enjoy an uncluttered interface focused purely on your media. No interstitials, no banners, and no unsolicited notifications.
• 100% Private & Offline: Keeva operates with zero internet permissions. Your personal files never leave your device.
• Elegant Modern Interface: Styled in an intuitive design system with adaptive Light, Dark, and AMOLED-friendly themes.

HOW IT WORKS:
Keeva uses Android's official Storage Access Framework (SAF) to let you grant read access directly to the media directory of your choice. You stay in control of your files at all times.
```
