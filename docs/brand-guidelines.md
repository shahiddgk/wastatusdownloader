# WA Status Downloader brand guidelines

## Product names

- Full product name: **WA Status Downloader**
- Short launcher name: **WA Saver**
- Internal application identity: `ratedwhats.appstatusdownloader`

Use the full product name in settings, release notes, documentation, and Android application information. Use the short name only where launcher space is constrained.

## Logo

The canonical launcher source is `assets/icon/launcher_icon.png`. It is a text-free green status ring with a white download arrow and a small play badge. Do not add another product name, publisher name, watermark, or copied mark.

The in-app logo uses `assets/icon/logo_full.png`.

## Color and typography

The product uses deep emerald surfaces, bright green accents, white foreground symbols, and Plus Jakarta Sans. Existing semantic colors in `lib/app/theme/app_colors.dart` are the implementation source of truth.

## Usage

- Keep the complete launcher symbol inside the Android adaptive icon safe area.
- Preserve aspect ratio.
- Keep launcher artwork text-free.
- Use **WA Status Downloader** exactly in public copy.
- State clearly that the product is independent from WhatsApp and Meta.
