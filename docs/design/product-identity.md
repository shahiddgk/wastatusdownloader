# Product identity

## Naming

The public product is **WA Status Downloader**. Android and iOS launchers use **WA Saver** where a shorter label is needed.

The Android application ID, Kotlin namespace, and iOS bundle identifier are:

```text
ratedwhats.appstatusdownloader
```

## Visual identity

The current icon uses a green status/chat ring, a white download arrow, and a small play badge. The text-free launcher source is `assets/icon/launcher_icon.png`; `assets/icon/logo_full.png` is available for larger in-app presentation.

The UI uses deep green and charcoal surfaces with bright green accents. Plus Jakarta Sans is bundled for product typography.

## Voice

Use plain action-oriented labels such as **Save**, **Share**, **Photos**, and **Videos**. Explain Android file access before requesting it. Do not imply that the app can identify a status sender automatically.

## Independence statement

WA Status Downloader is an independent utility and is not affiliated with or endorsed by WhatsApp or Meta.
